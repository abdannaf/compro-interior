## Project Overview

EcoNest Apartments is a boutique residential project in Semarang that celebrates the rich architectural heritage of Central Java while delivering contemporary living standards. The development features 24 thoughtfully designed apartments across 6 stories.

## Design Concept

The design language draws inspiration from traditional Javanese architecture — the emphasis on natural ventilation, connection with nature, and warm material palettes — reinterpreted through a modern minimalist lens.

- **Joglo-inspired ceiling details** in common areas reference traditional Javanese roof structures
- **Courtyard layout** creates natural ventilation corridors
- **Warm wood and batik textile accents** honor local craftsmanship
- **Minimalist furniture with organic curves** softens modern lines

## Interior Design Approach

Each apartment was designed with flexibility in mind:

- **Open-plan living and dining** with integrated kitchen islands
- **Sliding partition systems** allow bedrooms to open into the living area
- **Built-in storage** throughout eliminates the need for additional furniture
- **Balconies with privacy screens** made from laser-cut metal panels featuring batik patterns

## Common Area Design

The shared spaces reflect the project's commitment to community:

- **Ground-floor café and library** open to residents and the public
- **Rooftop community garden** with herb boxes and seating areas
- **Yoga and wellness room** with natural ventilation and timber flooring
- **Children's creative studio** with chalkboard walls and craft stations

## Local Craftsmanship

A key aspect of this project was supporting local artisans:

- Custom furniture crafted by Jepara woodworkers
- Hand-painted tiles from local ceramic artists
- Woven textile installations by Semarang textile cooperatives
- Metalwork features by local blacksmiths

## Result

EcoNest has become a benchmark for culturally-responsive design in Central Java. The project won the Indonesia Interior Design Award 2023 in the Residential category and achieved full occupancy within three months of completion.
