## Project Overview

Aurora Business Park is a flagship commercial development in the heart of Bandung's emerging business district. The project spans 12 stories of premium office space, retail areas, and communal facilities designed to attract forward-thinking enterprises.

## Design Concept

Our design approach centered on creating a workspace that promotes productivity while prioritizing occupant wellbeing. The building features:

- **Open-plan office floors** with floor-to-ceiling windows maximizing natural light
- **Biophilic design elements** including indoor gardens on every third floor
- **Collaborative spaces** with flexible furniture systems
- **A rooftop garden** offering panoramic views of the Bandung highlands

## Technical Details

The interior fitout covered over 15,000 square meters across all floors. Key technical achievements include:

- Energy-efficient HVAC systems integrated into ceiling design
- Acoustic paneling for noise reduction between zones
- Smart lighting systems with occupancy sensors
- Raised floor systems for flexible cable management

## Sustainability Features

Aurora Business Park achieved a Green Building certification with features including:

- Solar panels on the rooftop
- Rainwater harvesting systems
- Low-VOC materials throughout
- Native landscaping requiring minimal irrigation

## Result

The project was delivered on time and within budget, achieving 85% occupancy within the first six months. The building has been recognized as one of Bandung's most innovative commercial developments.
