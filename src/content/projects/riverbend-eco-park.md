## Project Overview

Riverbend Eco Park is a premium residential development in South Jakarta, designed for environmentally conscious families seeking modern luxury. The complex features 48 residential units ranging from 2-bedroom apartments to 4-bedroom penthouses.

## Design Concept

The project embraces the concept of "living with nature" — every design decision was made to minimize environmental impact while maximizing comfort and luxury.

- **Cross-ventilation design** reduces air conditioning dependency
- **Floor-to-ceiling windows** in all living areas blur the boundary between indoor and outdoor
- **Natural material palette** — timber, stone, and bamboo feature prominently
- **Private gardens** for ground-floor units and sky terraces for upper floors

## Interior Highlights

BuildPRO handled the complete interior design and fitout for all common areas and show units:

- **Grand lobby** with a living green wall spanning two stories
- **Resort-style pool deck** with cabanas and outdoor kitchen
- **Children's play area** with natural, non-toxic materials
- **Co-working lounge** for residents who work from home
- **Meditation garden** with water features and aromatic planting

## Material Selection

Every material was chosen for both aesthetics and sustainability:

- Reclaimed teak wood for feature walls and furniture
- Locally sourced natural stone for flooring
- Low-emission paints and finishes throughout
- Bamboo composite decking for outdoor areas

## Result

Riverbend Eco Park received the Indonesia Green Building Council certification and was featured in Architecture Digest Southeast Asia as a model for sustainable luxury living.
